- `Dataset002/analysis/build_dataset_002.py`

- `Dataset001/analysis/analyze_dataset_001.py`
- `Dataset002/analysis/analyze_dataset_002.py`

- Dataset 001: exact numeric and nonnumeric match.
- Dataset 002: exact schema and nonnumeric match; maximum numeric difference `8.881784197001252e-16`.
