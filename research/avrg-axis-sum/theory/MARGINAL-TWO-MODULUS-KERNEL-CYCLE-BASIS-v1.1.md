# Marginal Two-Modulus Kernel as a Cycle Space — v1.1

Status: proved research unit under `ACTIVE-RESEARCH-v1.1`.

## 1. Setup

Fix \(N\ge2\) and moduli \(r,s\ge1\). Let

\[
M_{N;(r,s)}=
\begin{pmatrix}
D_{N,r}\\
D_{N,s}
\end{pmatrix}
\]

act on \(V_N=\mathbb C^{N-1}\).

Associate to the indices \(a=1,\ldots,N-1\) the bipartite multigraph \(G_{N;r,s}\) whose left vertices are the realized residues

\[
U=\{2a-N\pmod r:1\le a\le N-1\},
\]

whose right vertices are

\[
V=\{2a-N\pmod s:1\le a\le N-1\},
\]

and whose edge \(e_a\) joins the two residues attached to \(a\).

The matrix of \(M_{N;(r,s)}\), after zero rows are removed, is the unsigned vertex-edge incidence matrix of \(G_{N;r,s}\).

## 2. Balanced edge flows

For \(w=(w_a)_{a=1}^{N-1}\in V_N\), the condition

\[
M_{N;(r,s)}w=0
\]

is equivalent to the pair of balance conditions

\[
\sum_{a:e_a\ni u}w_a=0
\qquad (u\in U),
\]

and

\[
\sum_{a:e_a\ni v}w_a=0
\qquad (v\in V).
\]

Thus invisible deformations are exactly complex edge weights whose unsigned sum vanishes at every vertex.

## 3. Alternating cycle vectors

Let

\[
C=(e_{a_1},e_{a_2},\ldots,e_{a_{2m}})
\]

be an even cycle in the bipartite graph. Define the alternating cycle vector \(z_C\in\mathbb C^{N-1}\) by

\[
(z_C)_{a_j}=(-1)^{j-1}
\]

for the edges of the cycle and zero elsewhere.

At each cycle vertex, the two incident cycle coefficients are \(+1\) and \(-1\). Hence

\[
M_{N;(r,s)}z_C=0.
\]

## Theorem 3.1 (kernel equals alternating cycle space)

The kernel of \(M_{N;(r,s)}\) is spanned by alternating cycle vectors of \(G_{N;r,s}\).

Equivalently,

\[
\ker M_{N;(r,s)}
=
\mathcal Z_{\mathrm{alt}}(G_{N;r,s}),
\]

where the right-hand side denotes the alternating edge-cycle space over \(\mathbb C\).

### Proof

Multiply every row belonging to the right vertex class by \(-1\). This invertible row operation changes the unsigned incidence matrix into an oriented incidence matrix \(B\), with every edge directed from the left class to the right class.

Therefore the kernel is unchanged. The kernel of an oriented incidence matrix is the ordinary circulation space of the graph. For a bipartite graph, every cycle has even length, and its oriented circulation vector becomes precisely an alternating \(+1,-1\) vector when translated back to the original unsigned convention. The standard cycle-space decomposition then proves the claim.

## Corollary 3.2 (kernel dimension)

If \(E=N-1\), \(n_V=|U|+|V|\), and \(c(G)\) is the number of connected components, then

\[
\dim\ker M_{N;(r,s)}
=
|E|-|U|-|V|+c(G).
\]

This agrees with rank-nullity and the previously proved formula

\[
\operatorname{rank}M_{N;(r,s)}
=|U|+|V|-c(G).
\]

## Theorem 3.3 (fundamental alternating-cycle basis)

Choose a spanning forest \(T\) of \(G_{N;r,s}\). For every edge \(e\notin T\), the graph \(T\cup\{e\}\) contains a unique cycle \(C_e\). Then

\[
\{z_{C_e}:e\in E\setminus T\}
\]

is a basis of \(\ker M_{N;(r,s)}\).

### Proof

The number of nonforest edges is

\[
|E|-(|U|+|V|-c(G)),
\]

which equals the kernel dimension. Each fundamental cycle vector has coefficient \(1\) or \(-1\) on its distinguished nonforest edge and every other fundamental vector has coefficient zero there. Hence these vectors are linearly independent. The dimension count completes the proof.

## 4. Exact injectivity criterion

### Corollary 4.1

The marginal operator \(M_{N;(r,s)}\) is injective if and only if \(G_{N;r,s}\) is a forest.

### Proof

Injectivity is equivalent to a zero-dimensional kernel. By Corollary 3.2 this holds exactly when

\[
|E|=|U|+|V|-c(G),
\]

which is the defining edge count for a forest.

## 5. Interpretation

The natural invisible deformations are not arbitrary vectors. They are generated by alternating transfers around residue-coupling cycles. Along a four-cycle, for example, adding

\[
(+t,-t,+t,-t)
\]

around the successive edges leaves every separate residue marginal unchanged.

This gives a direct geometric meaning to the kernel requested in Open Problem O2 of the frozen theory.
