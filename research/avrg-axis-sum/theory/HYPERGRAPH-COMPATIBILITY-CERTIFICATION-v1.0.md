# ACTIVE-003-R — Hypergraph Compatibility Certification

## Status

Validated exact-search strengthening for the certification-margin branch-and-bound layer.

## 1. Motivation

ACTIVE-003-Q associates to every still-certifiable channel `c` a mandatory closure

\[
\overline M_c\subseteq R,
\]

consisting of the not-yet-selected frequencies and precedence ancestors that must be added if the channel is to cross its certification threshold.

The pairwise compatibility graph joins channels `c,d` when

\[
|\overline M_c\cup\overline M_d|\le t,
\]

where `t` is the remaining exact budget. Every simultaneously certifiable channel family is therefore a clique in that graph.

Pairwise compatibility is necessary but not sufficient: all pairs in a family may fit within the budget while the full union does not.

## 2. Higher-order compatibility

For a channel family `T`, define its joint mandatory closure

\[
\mathcal M(T)=\bigcup_{c\in T}\overline M_c.
\]

The family is budget-feasible only if

\[
|\mathcal M(T)|\le t.
\]

A forbidden hyperedge is a channel family `E` satisfying

\[
|\mathcal M(E)|>t.
\]

Thus the feasible simultaneous certification families are precisely the vertex subsets containing no forbidden hyperedge generated by the mandatory closures.

## 3. Exact hypergraph upper bound

Let `V` be the channels that are already certified or remain individually certifiable at the current search node. Define

\[
UB_{\mathrm{hyp}}(S,X,t)
=
\max\left\{|T|:T\subseteq V,
\left|\bigcup_{c\in T}\overline M_c\right|\le t\right\}.
\]

For every completion `Q` of the current node, the channels certified by `S\cup Q` have a joint mandatory closure contained in `Q`; hence their union has size at most `t`. Therefore

\[
F(S\cup Q)\le UB_{\mathrm{hyp}}(S,X,t).
\]

This is an exact admissible upper bound.

## 4. Relation to the pairwise graph bound

Every hypergraph-feasible family is pairwise compatible, so

\[
UB_{\mathrm{hyp}}\le UB_{\mathrm{pair}}.
\]

The inequality can be strict when all pairs satisfy the budget but a triple or larger family does not.

## 5. Search integration

The branch-and-bound algorithm retains:

- transitive precedence propagation;
- the closure-ratio branching order from ACTIVE-003-O;
- exact-budget search;
- the independently checked local higher-prime-power contamination threshold.

At each node it computes the mandatory closure of every channel and replaces the pairwise clique upper bound by the exact maximum feasible channel-family cardinality under the union-cost constraint.

Because the benchmark uses budgets at most four and reduced channel counts are small, exact subset enumeration is used only inside the verifier. No polynomial-time claim is made.

## 6. Finite verification result

For

\[
4\le N\le120,
\qquad 3\le r\le30,
\qquad 0\le B\le4,
\]

there were `12,987` optimization cases.

The hypergraph-aware search:

- matched exhaustive optimum values in every case;
- produced zero false prime-pair certificates;
- reduced aggregate visited nodes from `65,209` to `65,191`;
- improved `9` cases;
- tied `12,978` cases;
- worsened `0` cases.

An explicit strict improvement occurs at

\[
N=44,\quad r=13,\quad q=13,\quad B=2.
\]

Both methods attain optimum `4` with selected frequencies `{1,3}`, while the pairwise-bound tree visits `9` nodes and the hypergraph-bound tree visits `7`.

## 7. Scientific classification

This unit establishes an exact finite-search strengthening and identifies genuine higher-order channel incompatibility beyond pairwise compatibility.

It does not establish:

- polynomial-time complexity;
- a uniform asymptotic speedup;
- a proof of Goldbach's conjecture;
- progress on RH or GRH.
