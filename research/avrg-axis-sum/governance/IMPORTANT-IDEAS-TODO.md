- Are generators differences of residue classes, reflection orbits, or short cycles?

### TODO-A3 — Stable restricted reconstruction

Required distinction: injectivity, stability, and statistical recovery are separate claims.

## Priority B — theory extensions after Paper 1 foundation

### TODO-B1 — Natural observable algebra

**Classification:** definition-and-relations program.

Define a restricted algebra generated by atomic valuation coordinates

\[
E_p^{(1)}(a)=v_p(a),\qquad E_p^{(2)}(a)=v_p(N-a),
\]

and determine which commonly used observables belong to it. Avoid the trivial algebra of all functions on a finite fiber.

Main task: identify relations, redundancy, and finite-coordinate truncation rules.

### TODO-B2 — Addition-rank observable

**Classification:** candidate observable and computational study.

For \(a+b=N\), define

\[
\rho_{\mathrm{add}}(a,N)
=|\operatorname{supp}(a)\cup\operatorname{supp}(b)|.
\]

Study its exact distribution on fibers and its relationship to prime-pair locations. It must remain an observable, not a claimed Goldbach mechanism, unless stronger evidence is obtained.

### TODO-B3 — Fiber signatures and collisions

**Classification:** invariant-design program.

Construct finite signatures from distributions of accepted observables and ask:

- when do distinct \(N,M\) have identical signatures?
- which observables separate fibers?
- how much information is lost under reflection-invariant signatures?

A signature is not called complete until injectivity is proved on an explicitly stated domain.

### TODO-B4 — Morphisms between fibers

**Classification:** research question.

Determine whether there are natural maps

\[
\Phi_{N,M}:\mathcal G_N\to\mathcal G_M
\]

that preserve specified structures such as reflection, residue channels, measures, or selected observables. Do not use category language until objects, morphisms, identities, and composition are all defined and verified.
