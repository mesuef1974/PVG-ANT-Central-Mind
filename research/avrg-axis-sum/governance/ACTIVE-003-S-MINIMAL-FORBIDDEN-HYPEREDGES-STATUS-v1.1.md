# ACTIVE-003-S — Minimal Forbidden Channel Hyperedges

Status: **CLOSED / PASS**

## Scope

This unit replaces the complete upward-closed family of forbidden channel sets by its inclusion-minimal antichain.

For channel mandatory closures \(\overline M_c\), a channel family \(T\) is forbidden when

\[
\left|\bigcup_{c\in T}\overline M_c\right|>t.
\]

It is minimal forbidden when every proper subset is feasible.

Every forbidden family contains a minimal forbidden subfamily. Therefore avoiding the minimal antichain is equivalent to avoiding the complete forbidden family, and the exact Hypergraph compatibility upper bound is unchanged.
