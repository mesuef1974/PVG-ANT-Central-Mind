# RMG-GOV-004 — Decision Rules

## Rule 1 — Correctness and originality are separate

A theorem may be correct while its novelty class is `CLASSICAL_RESULT_IN_RMG` or `PVG_REINTERPRETATION`.

## Rule 2 — Removal test controls PVG attribution

If removing valuation geometry leaves the statement and proof unchanged, the maximum default classification is `PVG-N1` unless a separately documented discovery or compression contribution justifies `PVG-N2` or `PVG-N3`.

## Rule 3 — Discovery contribution is allowed but bounded

A PVG-generated observable may be classified `PVG-N3` even when its final proof is classical. This does not automatically imply a new theorem or historical novelty.

## Rule 4 — High-risk open problems remain at MATH-M0

Goldbach, RH, GRH, and comparable open-problem translations remain `MATH-M0` unless a separately certified theorem changes the mathematical state of knowledge.

## Rule 5 — Finite verification is not mathematical promotion

Regression tests and numerical experiments can increase `ASSIM`, `OPS`, and `CERT`; they cannot by themselves increase `MATH`.

## Rule 6 — Prior-art uncertainty lowers the ceiling

When a result resembles standard linear algebra, contingency-table theory, algebraic statistics, Fourier analysis, or discrete tomography, its originality status remains `REQUIRES_TARGETED_AUDIT` until citations and overlap are resolved.

## Rule 7 — Pilot overlays do not rewrite history

Historical files remain untouched. The overlay is the active claim interpretation until a governed source-file migration is authorized.
