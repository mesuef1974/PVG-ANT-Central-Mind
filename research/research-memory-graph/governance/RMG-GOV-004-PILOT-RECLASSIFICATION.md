# RMG-GOV-004 — Retrospective Pilot Reclassification

Status: pilot_complete_non_exhaustive
Scope: five high-risk claim patterns only
Repository-wide completeness: NOT_CLAIMED

## Purpose

Apply the RMG-GOV-001 namespace separation and PVG necessity gate to a small, representative, high-risk sample before any repository-wide rewrite.

## Pilot sample

1. Multi-modulus exact-rank / Fourier-union theorem pattern.
2. Marginal-versus-joint information-gap theorem pattern.
3. Goldbach-as-fiber-intersection reformulation pattern.
4. PVG-generated observable pattern (`research/one-theorem/001` family).
5. RH/GRH-linked diagnostic or translation pattern.

## Reclassification principles

- A correct theorem may remain mathematically valid while being downgraded as a PVG contribution.
- Removal of valuation geometry without changing statement or proof implies at most `PVG-N1` unless a separate generative role is documented.
- Finite verification affects `ASSIM`, `OPS`, and `CERT`, never `MATH` automatically.
- Goldbach, RH, and GRH reformulations remain `MATH-M0` unless an independent theorem changes their known mathematical status.
- A PVG-generated observable can receive `PVG-N3` even when the final proof is classical, provided the observable's discovery path is documented and prior art remains under review.

## Pilot outcome

- One pattern is retained as a potentially substantive finite theorem but novelty remains unverified.
- One pattern is reclassified as a classical/statistical structure requiring prior-art integration.
- One pattern is classified as an exact reformulation with no theorem-level progress.
- One pattern is retained as a PVG-generated diagnostic/observable contribution.
- One pattern is constrained to diagnostic translation with explicit RH/GRH no-progress ceiling.

## Non-destructive policy

This report does not rewrite historical source files. Its decisions are stored as overlay records in `rmg-pilot-reclassification.jsonl`.

## Scientific ceiling

- no Goldbach proof;
- no RH or GRH progress;
- no blanket originality claim;
- no repository-wide compliance claim;
- no automatic retirement of historical material.
