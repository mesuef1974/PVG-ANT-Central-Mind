**Purpose:** route the existing referee packet to specialists without confusing priority review with proof review

## 1. Selection principles

A useful external review requires two independent competencies:

1. knowledge of squarefull / powerful / `k`-full number literature and arithmetic progressions;
2. expertise in Euler products, Dirichlet `L`-functions, Selberg–Delange methods, and contour arguments.

One person may cover both, but the project should prefer two independent reviews.

This reviewer need not specialize in squarefull numbers. Their role is to verify:

- uniformity and parameter dependence.

## 4. Conflict and independence rules

The project should avoid treating any of the following as a sufficient external certificate:

- another AI review;
- a collaborator who helped construct the proof;
- a reviewer checking only numerical examples;
- a priority opinion without reading the theorem statement;
- a proof review without checking cited prior art.

The minimum acceptable external closure is:

```text
one priority/terminology review
+
one independent line-by-line proof review
```
