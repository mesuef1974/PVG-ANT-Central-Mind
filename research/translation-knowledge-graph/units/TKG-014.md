# TKG-014 — Explicit-Formula Assembly Core

## Purpose

Build a governed assembly graph for explicit formulas without claiming a new derivation or analytic theorem.

The unit connects:

```text
arithmetic coefficients
→ Dirichlet series / logarithmic derivative
→ Perron or Mellin kernel
→ contour integrand
→ poles and zeros
→ residue contributions
→ endpoint and archimedean terms
→ certified remainder
```

## Canonical assembly schema

For a summatory or weighted observable, the structural target is

\[
\text{observable}
=
\text{main-pole terms}
+
\text{nontrivial-zero terms}
+
\text{trivial-zero terms}
+
\text{archimedean terms}
+
\text{endpoint terms}
+
\text{certified error}.
\]

This is an assembly schema, not a proof.

## Sharp Chebyshev target

A classical target shape for suitable conventions is

\[
\psi(x)
=
x-
\sum_{\rho}\frac{x^{\rho}}{\rho}
-
\log(2\pi)
-
\frac12\log(1-x^{-2}).
\]

The unit stores this only as a routing target. It does not derive it. Any use must state:

- the range of \(x\);
- the endpoint convention at prime powers;
- the ordering or truncation of the zero sum;
- normalization and branch conventions;
- the treatment of poles, trivial zeros, and the remainder.

## Weighted form

For a test function \(w\), the weighted prime-power observable

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}\Lambda(n)w(n/x)
\]

is routed through

\[
-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}\,\mathcal M w(s)x^s.
\]

A nontrivial zero \(\rho\) formally contributes a term proportional to

\[
-\mathcal M w(\rho)x^{\rho}.
\]

For the sharp Perron kernel, the corresponding formal term is

\[
-\frac{x^{\rho}}{\rho}.
\]

Formal contribution does not imply convergence, zero certification, or authorization.

## Authorization gate

Explicit-formula authorization remains blocked unless all of the following are verified:

1. coefficient identity;
2. kernel and transform;
3. meromorphic continuation in the required region;
4. functional equation when used;
5. complete pole and zero ledger;
6. residue computations;
7. contour orientation;
8. all segment bounds;
9. truncation and order of limits;
10. zero-sum convention;
11. endpoint convention;
12. archimedean terms;
13. attached proof or authoritative source.

Even syntactic completion of these fields does not establish the evidence.

## ANT ↔ PVG translation

### Forward

Prime-power valuation points provide the coefficient geometry:

\[
\nu(n)=k e_p
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Lambda(n)=\log p.
\]

A global weight gives

\[
(\log p)\,w(p^k/x).
\]

### Reverse limitation

An aggregated explicit-formula value does not reconstruct the original list of prime powers or valuation points. Complex zero geometry, Gamma factors, and contour estimates are not encoded by finite-prime valuation geometry alone.

Therefore the translation is:

```text
hybrid
nonlocal
lossy after aggregation
```

## Counterexamples and refusal rules

The reasoning core rejects:

```text
listing zeros ⇒ convergence of the zero sum
functional-equation symmetry ⇒ RH or GRH
correct main term ⇒ certified error term
smooth kernel ⇒ arithmetic cancellation
finite zero truncation ⇒ exact explicit formula
```
