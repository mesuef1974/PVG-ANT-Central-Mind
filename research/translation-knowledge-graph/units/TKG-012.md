## 1. Purpose

TKG-011 introduced Perron routing for sharp summatory functions. TKG-012 adds the smoother multiplicative-transform layer

\[
\mathcal M w(s)=\int_0^\infty w(u)u^{s-1}\,du,
\]

and the formal weighted route

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}a(n)w(n/x)
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{(c)}F(s)\mathcal Mw(s)x^s\,ds,
\qquad
F(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{a(n)}{n^s},
\]

only when the common strip, interchange, inversion, and vertical-integrability hypotheses are justified.

## 2. Why smoothing matters

A sharp cutoff records exact membership in \(n\le x\), but its transform has limited decay. Replacing it by \(w(n/x)\) can improve analytic behavior by trading exact boundary information for regularity.

The unit distinguishes three built-in weights.

### Sharp cutoff

\[
w(u)=\mathbf 1_{0<u\le1},
\qquad
\mathcal Mw(s)=\frac1s.
\]

It preserves the exact cutoff but has a jump at \(u=1\).

### Triangular weight

\[
w(u)=\max(1-u,0),
\qquad
\mathcal Mw(s)=\frac{1}{s(s+1)}.
\]

It is continuous and compactly supported, but only piecewise smooth.

### Exponential weight

\[
w(u)=e^{-u},
\qquad
\mathcal Mw(s)=\Gamma(s),
\quad \Re(s)>0.
\]

It is smooth and rapidly decreasing, but every positive integer contributes; the cutoff is no longer exact.

## 3. Weighted arithmetic observables

For the constant coefficients,

\[
S_w(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}w(n/x)
\]

is routed through

\[
\zeta(s)\mathcal Mw(s)x^s.
\]

For von Mangoldt coefficients,

\[
\Psi_w(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}\Lambda(n)w(n/x)
\]

is routed through

\[
-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}\mathcal Mw(s)x^s.
\]

For exponential smoothing this becomes the formal integrand

\[
-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}\Gamma(s)x^s.
\]

This is an assembly route, not a PNT proof.

## 4. Decay/regularity ledger

The mind must not infer transform decay from a label such as “smooth” without recording the actual hypotheses. Each weight requires:

```text
support
behavior near 0
behavior at infinity
number of derivatives
endpoint vanishing
fundamental strip
claimed vertical decay
proof or citation
```

The reasoning rule is conservative:

> Repeated integration by parts can create additional vertical decay only when derivatives exist, are integrable in the required weighted sense, and boundary terms vanish or are explicitly controlled.

Counterexamples:

- compact support without smoothness does not imply arbitrary rapid decay;
- a jump discontinuity obstructs rapid transform decay;
- a rapidly decaying kernel does not supply a zero-free region for \(\zeta\) or \(L(s,\chi)\).

## 5. ANT ↔ PVG translation

For \(n=\operatorname{decode}(\alpha)\), the smoothed observable weights a PVG point by

\[
w\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{decode}(\alpha)}x\right).
\]

For von Mangoldt,

\[
\nu(n)=ke_p
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(\log p)\,w(p^k/x).
\]

This translation preserves labelled prime-power support, the arithmetic coefficient, the decoded scale, and the chosen kernel. After summation, individual points are generally not recoverable from one scalar observable.

The operation is therefore:

```text
hybrid
nonlocal in valuation coordinates
scale-sensitive
not a coordinatewise axis operator
```
