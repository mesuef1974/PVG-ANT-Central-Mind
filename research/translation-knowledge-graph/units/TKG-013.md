## 2. Standard residue definitions

For a meromorphic function `G` near `s0`,

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{s=s_0}G(s)
\]

is the coefficient of `(s-s0)^{-1}` in the Laurent expansion.

For a simple pole,

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{s=s_0}G(s)
=
\lim_{s\to s_0}(s-s_0)G(s).
\]

Equivalently, for a small positively oriented circle `C` around `s0`,

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{s=s_0}G(s)
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_C G(s)\,ds.
\]

### Numerical/symbolic examples

For the Perron kernel,

\[
G(s)=\frac{x^s}{s},
\]

we have

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{s=0}\frac{x^s}{s}=1.
\]

If `F(s)` has a simple pole at `s0` with residue `r`, then

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{s=s_0}\left(F(s)x^s\right)=r x^{s_0}.
\]

### Counterexamples and failure modes

- Direct substitution into a pole is invalid.
- An apparent pole may be cancelled by a zero in another factor.
- A kernel pole and an arithmetic pole must not be merged without checking order and cancellation.

## 3. Rectangular contour orientation

For a counterclockwise rectangle with right edge `Re(s)=c`, left edge `Re(s)=sigma`, and height `T`, the traversal is:

```text
right:  upward
top:    leftward
left:   downward
bottom: rightward
```

Thus

\[
I_{\rm right}^{\uparrow}
+
I_{\rm top}^{\leftarrow}
+
I_{\rm left}^{\downarrow}
+
I_{\rm bottom}^{\rightarrow}
=
2\pi i\sum \operatorname{Res}.
\]

Since

\[
I_{\rm left}^{\downarrow}=-I_{\rm left}^{\uparrow},
\]

we obtain the shift identity

\[
I_{\rm right}^{\uparrow}
=
I_{\rm left}^{\uparrow}
+2\pi i\sum\operatorname{Res}
-I_{\rm top}^{\leftarrow}
-I_{\rm bottom}^{\rightarrow}.
\]

This orientation ledger is mandatory because a large fraction of contour errors are sign errors rather than deep analytic errors.

## 5. Error-term ledger

A contour argument must separately record:

```text
right truncation tail
left vertical integral
top horizontal integral
bottom horizontal integral
small indentation arcs
endpoint half-weight
zero-sum truncation
limit interchange
```

Each error component must identify:

```text
parameter
domain
norm or absolute value used
bound
uniformity range
limiting order
proof or source
```

The notation `O(error)` without a defined parameter and range is rejected.

## 6. Worked routing for Perron-type integrands

For

\[
G(s)=F(s)\frac{x^s}{s},
\]

potential contributions include:

1. poles of `F`;
2. the kernel pole at `s=0`;
3. poles created by logarithmic derivatives at zeros of the underlying function;
4. trivial-zero and archimedean contributions;
5. indentation terms when a singularity lies on the contour.

For

\[
F(s)=-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)},
\]

one must audit the outer minus sign before assigning the residue at a zero `rho`. A formal list of zeros is not enough; signs and multiplicities must be checked.

## 7. ANT ↔ PVG translation

The translation is hybrid and nonlocal.

PVG contributes:

```text
coefficient provenance
prime-power support
labelled axes
finite arithmetic observables
```

Complex analysis contributes:

```text
Laurent expansions
residues
contour geometry
segment estimates
limiting processes
```

Therefore:

```text
PVG coefficient geometry
≠ residue theorem
≠ contour bound
≠ explicit formula
```

After contour aggregation, the resulting scalar observable generally does not retain the identity of individual valuation points.
