# TKG-005 — Chebyshev Observables, Summatory Transforms, and Partial Summation

## Status

`IMPLEMENTED — VERIFIER COMMITTED — EXECUTION NOT CLAIMED`

## Purpose

Move the Translation Knowledge Graph from local coefficients to cumulative arithmetic observables without confusing exact finite sums with asymptotic prime-distribution theorems.

## Core objects

- `pi(x)=#{p<=x}`;
- `theta(x)=sum_{p<=x} log p`;
- `psi(x)=sum_{n<=x} Lambda(n)=sum_{p^k<=x} log p`;
- the exact prime-power correction between `psi` and `theta`;
- Abel/partial summation as the governed bridge between weighted and unweighted sums.

## PVG interpretation

```text
pi(x)    = count unit-height labelled single-axis points up to x
theta(x) = log-axis mass on those unit-height points
psi(x)   = log-axis mass on every positive-height single-axis point up to x
```

This translation is exact before scalar aggregation. The scalar cumulative values lose the individual locations unless the underlying point list is retained.

## Numerical example: x=10

```text
primes: 2,3,5,7
pi(10)=4
theta(10)=log2+log3+log5+log7=log210
psi terms: 2,3,4,5,7,8,9
psi(10)=3log2+2log3+log5+log7
psi(10)-theta(10)=2log2+log3
```

The difference comes from the higher prime powers `4,8,9`.

## Partial summation route

For `A(t)=sum_{n<=t} a_n`,

```text
sum_{n<=x} a_n f(n)
= A(x)f(x) - integral_1^x A(t)f'(t)dt
```

under the standard regularity hypotheses. In particular, one may transfer information between `theta` and `pi`, but the quality of the transferred estimate depends on the quality of the input estimate and the kernel. Partial summation does not create an asymptotic theorem from a finite table.
