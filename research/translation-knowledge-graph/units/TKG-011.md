# TKG-011 — Perron Transforms, Contour-Shift Certificates, Residues, and Explicit-Formula Assembly

Status: COMPLETE AS ASSIM REASONING UNIT

## Purpose

TKG-011 builds the first controlled bridge from a Dirichlet series to a summatory arithmetic observable. It does not authorize an explicit formula merely because a contour integral can be written formally.

## Standard objects

For coefficients `a(n)`, define

\[
F(s)=\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{a(n)}{n^s},
\qquad
A(x)=\sum_{n\le x}a(n).
\]

A Perron-type route represents or approximates `A(x)` using an integral of

\[
F(s)\frac{x^s}{s}.
\]

The exact theorem depends on truncation, regularity, convergence, and endpoint conventions.

## Reasoning rule

```text
Dirichlet coefficients
→ Dirichlet series
→ Perron kernel
→ initial vertical integral
→ certified contour shift
→ residues + new contour + boundary/error terms
→ summatory statement
```

Every arrow carries hypotheses.

## Example 1: constant coefficients

For

\[
F(s)=\zeta(s),\qquad a(n)=1,
\]

the target is

\[
A(x)=\lfloor x\rfloor.
\]

The pole at `s=1` supplies the candidate main contribution `x`. This observation alone is not a proof of the complete Perron formula or its error term.

## Example 2: von Mangoldt coefficients

For

\[
F(s)=-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)},
\]

the target is

\[
\psi(x)=\sum_{n\le x}\Lambda(n).
\]

Candidate singularity sources include:

- `s=1`;
- nontrivial zeros `rho` of zeta;
- trivial zeros;
- `s=0`, depending on the kernel and normalization.

This is the assembly graph required before an explicit formula; it is not itself an explicit-formula proof.

## Contour-shift certificate

A contour shift is blocked until the following fields are supplied and proved:

1. meromorphic continuation region;
2. complete pole ledger;
3. relevant zero and trivial-zero ledger;
4. vertical-side estimate;
5. horizontal-side estimates;
6. truncation error;
7. signed residue calculation;
8. orientation convention;
9. cutoff or endpoint convention.

A syntactically complete certificate still requires mathematical proofs for every field.

## Counterexample to formal reasoning

The statement

```text
the integrand is meromorphic, therefore the contour may be shifted arbitrarily
```

is false. A shift may cross unrecorded singularities, and the new boundary integrals may fail to vanish or even fail to converge.

## ANT ↔ PVG translation

A labelled valuation point `alpha=nu(n)` can be decoded to `n`. Perron then selects points according to the global inequality `n<=x`.

The translation therefore preserves:

- the coefficient attached to each decoded point;
- multiplicative size through `n^{-s}`;
- the summatory cutoff.

It is not purely local in valuation coordinates. The ordering `n<=x`, complex integration, poles, and boundary growth are global analytic structures.

Thus:

```text
PVG coefficient geometry
+ integer decoding
+ complex analytic transform
```

is required.
