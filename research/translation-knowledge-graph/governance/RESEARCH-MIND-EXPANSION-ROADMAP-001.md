# RESEARCH-MIND-EXPANSION-ROADMAP-001

Status: ACTIVE GOVERNANCE ROADMAP

## Purpose

This roadmap fixes the next phase of the PVG–ANT Central Mind. Expansion means deeper reasoning, stricter hypotheses, executable verification, and research diagnosis—not merely more files or more terminology.

## Governing equation

```text
research mind = knowledge + reasoning + verification + discovery discipline
```

No component may substitute for another.

## Expansion principles

1. Convert every concept into an applicable reasoning rule with triggers, hypotheses, transformations, failure modes, and claim ceiling.
2. Build multi-hop reasoning across TKG units rather than isolated registries.
3. Maintain a central hypothesis ledger covering convergence, coprimality, primitivity, regularity, uniformity, exceptional cases, and boundary terms.
4. Strengthen refusal rules. Finite evidence, symmetry, Euler products, and PVG reinterpretations do not automatically produce theorems.
5. Develop PVG-native objects: support geometry, height geometry, divisor boxes, additive fibers, residue fibers, and labelled-axis invariants.
6. Attach examples, boundary cases, and counterexamples to every major rule.
7. Formalize finite algebraic layers in Lean before attempting deep analytic formalization.
8. Use computation for diagnostics, identity testing, counterexample search, and experiment design—not as proof.

## Source priorities

### ANT analytic backbone

- Perron transforms.
- Mellin transforms and smoothing.
- Residue calculus and contour-shift certificates.
- Explicit formulas.
- Tauberian interfaces.
- Zero-free and zero-density diagnostics.
- Large sieve, Vaughan identities, and circle-method routing after the backbone is stable.

### PVG-native development

- support and height invariants;
- divisor-box operators;
- additive fibers `x+y=N` without false linearization of valuations;
- residue fibers and character spectra;
- preservation/loss ledgers for every ANT ↔ PVG translation.
