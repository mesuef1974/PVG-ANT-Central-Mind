- schema defaults;
- `MATH-M0` inserted by an adapter;
- synthetic OPS or CERT placeholders;
- filename heuristics;
- field-pattern heuristics;
- benchmark fixtures;
- an enlarged orchestrator;
- automatic type inference presented as canonical classification.

## Required ordering

1. Build a human-review inventory by record class.
2. Separate non-applicable structural records from substantive records.
3. Record authored, aliased, normalized, generated, and absent values independently for every governance axis.
4. Obtain human-authored classifications or explicit `NOT_AUTHORED` states.
5. Repair the adapter and verifier only after the authorship policy is fixed.
6. Re-audit a reviewed pilot before any TKG migration.
