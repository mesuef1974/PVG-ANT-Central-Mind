## Master walls

1. Parity wall: sieves count almost-primes better than primes.
2. Minor-arc wall: structure on major arcs must be matched by cancellation on minor arcs.
3. Zero-free wall: strong distribution errors require zero-free regions.
4. Siegel-zero wall: possible exceptional zeros create ineffectivity and bias.
5. RH/GRH wall: explicit formulas transfer the problem to zeros; they do not locate them.
6. Artin wall: Artin L-functions require holomorphy and zero-control.

## PVG transfer rules

1. No observable without its Dirichlet series or a clear reason none exists.
2. No Euler product without correct axes: primes, prime powers, prime ideals, or Frobenius fibers.
3. No main term without naming the pole and the analytic function.
4. No strong error without zero-free, zero-density, or an explicit hypothesis.
5. No RH-related positivity unless the full Weil explicit formula is represented.
6. Geometric relabeling does not replace zero-control.
