# Knowledge Radar Policy v1

**ID:** KNOWLEDGE-RADAR-POLICY-V1 (Stage Review 001 PR-A)

Task-triggered acquisition can starve discovery of tools the mind does not
know it needs. The radar closes that gap without recreating book mining.

## Allowed output (only)

- bibliographic pointers (exact source, section, claimed relevance);
- candidate knowledge gaps;
- candidate questions for the idea funnel.

## Forbidden

- operational translation cards;
- broad or untargeted book mining;
- knowledge activation, claims, or classifications above Diagnostic;
- any RH/GRH-adjacent expansion.

## Cycle

Once per research cycle, a bounded scan of recent results and directly
relevant chapters produces a stamped pointer list. A pointer activates into
real acquisition only when bound to a named task or a named failure, through
the sixteen-field common-language contract.

Zero RH progress; zero GRH progress.

**Classification:** Governance / Knowledge acquisition policy. No mathematical claim.
