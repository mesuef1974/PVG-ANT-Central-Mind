## Selection scorecard

Each candidate receives `0–5` on:

- exact statement quality;
- PVG necessity;
- literature gap plausibility;
- proof tractability;
- analytic relevance;
- falsifiability;
- negative-result value.

Automatic rejection occurs when:

- the statement is a direct classical identity;
- PVG can be removed without changing the proof strategy;
- a finite example is being mistaken for a theorem;
- the result is RH/GRH-equivalent or requires an unnamed major certificate;
- the statement lacks a precise quantifier/range;
- the relevant literature has not been checked.
