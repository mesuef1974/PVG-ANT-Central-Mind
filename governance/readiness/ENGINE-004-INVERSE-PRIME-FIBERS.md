\[
\mathcal R_2(N)=\{\{p,q\}:p<q,\ p+q=N\},
\]

## Exact routing laws

- If `N` is odd, any two-prime representation must be `2 + (N-2)`, hence multiplicity is at most one.
- If `N` is even, any distinct-prime representation uses two odd primes.
- Representation multiplicity is not determined by support alone.
