## Exact routing laws

- For `N in N(F)`, `N` is even if and only if axis `2` belongs to `F`.
- If `2 not in F`, the fiber is empty or the singleton `{2,N-2}`; multiplicity is at most one.
- If `2 in F`, every distinct-prime representation uses two odd primes.
- Support controls the parity route but does not determine multiplicity.

## Exact centered-gap laws

For `Delta=q-p`:

```text
p = (N-Delta)/2
q = (N+Delta)/2
Delta = N (mod 2)
N^2 - Delta^2 = 4pq
gcd(N,Delta) = gcd(N,2)
```

For even `N`, with `m=N/2` and `d=Delta/2`:

```text
p=m-d
q=m+d
m^2-d^2=pq
gcd(m,d)=1
```

These are elementary exact identities. No originality claim is attached.
