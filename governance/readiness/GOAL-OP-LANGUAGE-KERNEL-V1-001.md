## 1. Research question

Can the Central Mind stabilize a reusable bidirectional PVG–ANT language kernel consisting of eight canonical bridge families, each with an exact forward map, reverse map or information-loss statement, analytic transform, simplification-gain classification, finite example, and honest certificate?

## 2. Required output

```text
language release | bridge audit | structural certificates | negative classifications
```

This task does not seek an original theorem. It prepares the controlled language needed for the following Original Lemma Selection stage.

## 3. Success and stop rules

- **Success criterion:** eight declared bridge families pass the kernel audit and are reusable without rebuilding their foundations.
- **Negative/blocked criterion:** a bridge lacks an exact map, loses unstated information, has no valid analytic transform, duplicates a classical object without structural gain, or cannot be supported by a canonical example.
- **Claim ceiling:** L1 exact translation and certified L2 structural gain; an entry reaches L3 only through a proved transfer lemma with a genuine analytic consequence.
- **Explicitly out of scope:** Dataset 004, model retuning, new RH/GRH-equivalent fronts, OP-005 DS-3, Nyman–Beurling production, new zeta computation, broad book mining, and Lean formalization of elementary identities.

## 4. PVG–ANT route

- **Classical ANT object:** multiplication, divisibility, convolution, multiplicative observables, squarefree/Möbius structure, residue fibers, sieve information, and local/global analytic transforms.
- **Multiplicative core:** unique factorization and prime-power coordinate data.
- **PVG encoding:** `n ↦ ν(n)` in the finitely supported valuation lattice.
- **Geometric decomposition sought:** coordinate order, divisor boxes, support strata, truncated valuation profiles, residue fibers, and local-to-global coordinate factorization.
- **Analytic transform:** Dirichlet convolution/series, Euler factors, character Fourier transforms, sieve main-term/remainder certificates, contour/Tauberian transfer.
- **Candidate transfer lemma:** only where an exact geometric statement has a proved analytic consequence; otherwise classification stops at L1/L2.
- **Reverse translation to ANT:** every entry must restate its consequence in classical arithmetic language.
- **PVG-necessity test:** identify whether PVG linearizes, decomposes, localizes, compresses, transfers, or materially improves proof economy; otherwise mark the gain `none` or `expository`.

## 10. Failure points

- treating a geometric rename as structural gain;
- omitting additive order, modular labels, phase, convergence, or boundary loss;
- attributing a classical identity to PVG;
- certifying an analytic transform without its hypotheses;
- allowing external legacy material to become a parallel source of truth;
- expanding the stage into an open-problem front.
