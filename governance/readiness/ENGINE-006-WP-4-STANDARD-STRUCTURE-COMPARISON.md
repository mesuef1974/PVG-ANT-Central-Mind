## Required honesty tests

1. A geometric name is not evidence of a new object.
2. A finite certificate is not evidence of a new general structure.
3. Combining two standard structures is not automatically a new theory.
4. Organizational value must be distinguished from theorem-producing value.
5. Any claimed strict increase in information must exhibit data not reconstructible from the antecedent.
6. Any unresolved difference must be stated as a precise comparison question, not a slogan.

## Candidate expected outcomes

```text

centered coordinates = standard sum-difference parametrization
incidence geometry = standard bipartite relation
combined typed stack = useful synthesis but not an independent primitive structure
```

These are candidates, not precommitted conclusions.
