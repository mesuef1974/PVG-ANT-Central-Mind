# PVG Understanding Deepening 001

## Purpose

Deepen the Central Mind's native understanding of prime-valuation geometry before adding more translation cards or opening another theorem target.

The pass builds a formal working language for

\[
\nu(n)=(v_p(n))_{p\in\mathbb P}\in\mathbb N^{(\mathbb P)}
\]

and its interfaces with analytic number theory.

## Scope

1. Define the core PVG object grammar.
2. Define admissible morphisms and projections.
3. Record exact reconstruction conditions and information-loss levels.
4. Define composition rules between geometry and ANT transforms.
5. Separate labeled, unlabeled, local, aggregate, phase, and analytic data.
6. Add deterministic examples and counterexamples.
7. Build a future adversarial Benchmark 002 from compositions, not card-name recall.

## Non-goals

- no second theorem target;
- no Dataset 004;
- no RH/GRH claim;
- no claim that terminology creates novelty;
- no L3 promotion from ontology or routing knowledge;
- no broad book mining.

## Exit criteria

- at least 18 canonical PVG objects;
- at least 20 canonical morphisms or projections;
- explicit reconstruction lattice;
- explicit composition table with ANT interfaces;
- deterministic audit of examples and non-injectivity witnesses;
- readiness decision for Adversarial Benchmark 002.

## Classification

Capability maturation / language and reasoning infrastructure only.
