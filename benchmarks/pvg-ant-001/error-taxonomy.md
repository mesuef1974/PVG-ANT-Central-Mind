# PVG–ANT Benchmark 001 — Error Taxonomy

## E1 — Reverse-map overreach
A forward identity is valid, but the inverse is nonunique or requires labels that were discarded.

## E2 — Summary or aggregation mistaken for reconstruction
A scalar count, norm, moment, or sieve aggregate is treated as though it recovered the underlying valuation profiles.

## E3 — Phase, sign, or label erasure
Fourier energy, support counts, or unlabeled exponent multisets are used to infer residue location, sign cancellation, or prime identity.

## E4 — Formal local factor mistaken for an analytic theorem
A Bell series or formal Euler product is used without convergence, continuation, noncancellation, or growth certificates.

## E5 — Smooth/sharp transfer confusion
Mellin and Perron normalizations are mixed, or a smooth theorem is desmoothed without a boundary certificate.

## E6 — Fixed-parameter theorem promoted to uniform theorem
Dependence on q, r, conductor, strip width, or the weight is hidden.

## E7 — Mean-square information promoted to pointwise control
An L2 or averaged result is treated as a uniform bound.

## E8 — Probabilistic model promoted to integer theorem
Local independence or a Kubilius model is used without a quantitative transfer theorem.

## E9 — Classical method or identity promoted to originality
A new name, diagram, or application is confused with a new theorem or a new analytic method.

## E10 — PVG decorative rather than material
The geometric language supplies no invariant, simplification, route, certificate, or counterexample that was absent classically.
